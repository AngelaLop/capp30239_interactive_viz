3 Age

What is the main activity of the population in Panama according to their age?

This interactive visualization explores how participation in key life cycle activities varies across Panama's territories. Use the age slider to see how activity patterns change across different ages, and filter by sex to compare gender differences.

The map shows the dominant activity for each municipality or province, color-coded by activity type. Click on any territory to see detailed activity breakdowns in the charts below.

Activity Definitions

Students: Population who reported attending an educational institution at the time of the census.

Working Students: Population who reported both studying and working at the time of the interview.

Workers (Low income): Employed population who reported monthly earnings below the median.

Workers (High income): Employed population who reported median or higher monthly earnings.

Unemployed: Population who reported actively searching for a job at the time of the interview.

Inactive: Population not employed, not actively looking for work, and not studying or in training.

Retired: Population who are retired with pension access.
